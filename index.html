Who I am

One person.
Three languages.
One instinct.

I write, design, and make people laugh — in German, English, and Spanish. I'm based in Germany with a Master's in North & Latin American Studies, years in film production, a graphic design practice, a podcast I host, and a side life performing stand-up comedy in Spanish.

Before focusing on creative production, I spent years in film — writing love letters and fairy tale books as props, styling sets, managing budgets. That shaped how I think: every word and every visual either serves the story or it doesn't.

I also run Hankemacht — a curated marketplace for licensed film graphics, built for productions that need the right visual in the right format, fast.
